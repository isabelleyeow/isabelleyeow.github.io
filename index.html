Interactive Experience Research Project

Analysing Luni.com

Isabelle Yeow Jia Qi // s4160554

Analysis Questions:

1. What was the first thing you paid attention to when interacting with the experience?

The first thing I noticed was how interactive the whole scene felt, nearly every object shifted or reacted the moment my cursor hovered and passed over it. I realised that certain objects went further than a hover-reaction as clicking them triggered a short animated action, this drew me towards the website even more and I was interested what else I could discover.

2. Spend two minutes with the experience and create a list of each of your discrete actions.

  1. Moved the cursor from left to right to view the different perspectives of the landing page
  2. Hovered over every different section of the homepage to watch each "burst" of elements
  3. Clicked the pen and book at the back of the experience
  4. Hovered over the careers tab on the top right to see it change
  5. Clicked the first virtual world on the left
  6. Moved my cursor around and clicked on the balls
  7. Tried to shoot the basketball and football into the goals
  8. Scrolled down
  9. Pressed and clicked every object I could see
  10. Scrolled down further
  11. Clicked the punching bag multiple times
  12. Clicked everything else to see if it moved, like the egg in the pan
  13. Scrolled back to the main page and clicked the last section
  14. Swiped to see all the pictures before going back to home
